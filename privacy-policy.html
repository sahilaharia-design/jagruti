Privacy Policy

How Jagruti Steels Pvt. Ltd. collects, uses, stores and protects information submitted through jagrutisteels.com.

Effective Date: 17 August 2026 · Last Updated: 17 August 2026

Jagruti Steels Pvt. Ltd. ("Jagruti Steels," "we," "us," "our") operates jagrutisteels.com and its language versions (jagrutisteels.com/ru/, /hi/, /gu/) (together, the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you use the Site, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices available to you. By submitting information through our forms, chat assistant, or by otherwise using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Scope of This Policy

This policy covers information collected through jagrutisteels.com and its subdirectories, including the enquiry and catalogue-request forms, the on-site chat assistant, and standard website analytics. It does not cover information you provide to us through offline channels (phone calls, in-person meetings, courier-delivered documents) or third-party platforms we are listed on (IndiaMART, TradeIndia, ExportersIndia, JustDial, Google Business), which have their own privacy terms.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Enquiry form: name, company name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, product required, and free-text requirement details.
  • Catalogue request form: name, company name, business email, phone/WhatsApp number, and business type.
  • Chat assistant: name and phone number when you choose to submit a lead through the chat widget, plus whatever product category or free-text message you type into it.
  • Direct contact: anything you share with us by email, WhatsApp, or phone when you reach out directly using the contact details on the Site.

Information collected automatically

  • Standard analytics data via Google Analytics (GA4) — pages visited, time on page, approximate location derived from IP address, device and browser type, and referring website.
  • We do not use the Site to collect payment card details, government ID numbers, or other sensitive personal data, and we ask that you not include such information in free-text form fields.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry, quote request, or catalogue request, and to follow up by email, phone, or WhatsApp.
  • To email you the product catalogue (PDF) when requested.
  • To maintain an internal record of buyer enquiries for our own sales coordination (stored in a private Google Sheet accessible only to Jagruti Steels staff).
  • To understand how visitors use the Site, so we can improve content, load times, and navigation.
  • To meet legal, accounting, tax, and export-documentation obligations connected to a resulting order.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services We Use

To operate the Site and process enquiries, we rely on the following third-party services, each of which processes data under its own privacy terms:

  • Formspree (formspree.io) — receives and emails us the contents of the enquiry and catalogue-request forms.
  • Google Apps Script / Google Sheets — an internal spreadsheet-based CRM we use to log and track buyer enquiries. Access is restricted to Jagruti Steels staff.
  • Google Analytics (GA4) — website traffic and behaviour analytics, operated by Google LLC.
  • Google Maps — embedded maps showing our Mumbai and Gujarat facility locations on the Contact section of the homepage.
  • WhatsApp Business (Meta Platforms, Inc.) — when you click a WhatsApp link on the Site, your conversation with us continues on WhatsApp under WhatsApp's own privacy terms.
  • Vercel — our website hosting provider, which processes standard server logs (IP address, request timestamp, user agent) as part of delivering the Site.

5. Cookies & Analytics

The Site uses cookies set by Google Analytics (GA4) to distinguish visitors and measure site usage. These are standard analytics cookies — we do not run third-party advertising or retargeting pixels on this Site. You can block or delete analytics cookies at any time through your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Blocking cookies will not affect your ability to browse the Site or submit an enquiry.

6. Data Retention

We retain enquiry and catalogue-request data in our internal CRM for as long as reasonably necessary to manage the buyer relationship, respond to follow-up questions, and meet accounting or export-documentation requirements for any resulting order — typically for the duration of an active commercial relationship plus a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping. You may request earlier deletion at any time (see Section 9).

7. How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only with:

  • The third-party service providers listed in Section 4, strictly to operate the Site and process your enquiry;
  • Our own staff, on a need-to-know basis, to respond to and fulfil your enquiry or order;
  • Government authorities, customs bodies, or freight forwarders, where necessary to complete an export shipment you have ordered;
  • Legal or regulatory authorities, where required by applicable law.

8. International Data Transfers

We are based in Mumbai, India, and our Gujarat unit (Mammal Industries, Sarigam) is also based in India. Some of the third-party service providers listed in Section 4 (Formspree, Google, Vercel) operate infrastructure outside India, which means information you submit may be processed on servers located in other countries. We select providers that maintain industry-standard security and privacy practices for cross-border data handling.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of the personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to receive a copy of your data in a portable format. This applies to visitors from India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and to visitors from the European Union / European Economic Area under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), as well as buyers from other jurisdictions with equivalent protections. To exercise any of these rights, email info.jagrutisteels@gmail.com with your request — we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold — including restricted access to our internal CRM spreadsheet and use of reputable, security-conscious third-party providers. No method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

11. Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for business use by procurement professionals, buyers, and adults acting on behalf of a company. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, the services we use, or applicable law. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Material changes will be reflected here; we encourage you to review this page periodically.
