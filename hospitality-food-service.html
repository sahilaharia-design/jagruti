OEM Customisation

Your Format. Your Dimensions.
Our Manufacturing.

Share a drawing, equipment fitment, physical sample or target dimensions. We evaluate the material, tooling, forming and finishing route, prepare an approval sample and then move to repeat bulk production.

01
Size & Diameter
Manufactured to your outer diameter, depth and overall footprint — matched to equipment fitment or table-service format, not a fixed catalogue size.
02
Geometry
Basket profile, centre-bowl proportion, rim detail and overall form developed from your drawing, a physical sample or a dimensioned sketch.
03
Material
Material and gauge selection evaluated against your forming, durability and cost requirement once the specification is confirmed.
04
Mesh & Perforation
Mesh aperture, wire gauge and perforation pattern developed to your drainage, airflow or presentation requirement.
05
Handle & Rim Detail
Handle length and mounting, or rim profile and edge finish, built to your equipment interface or service-format requirement.
06
Finish, Branding & Supply
Finish options, logo embossing or branding subject to tooling feasibility, plus packaging and repeat bulk-supply scheduling for ongoing programmes.
