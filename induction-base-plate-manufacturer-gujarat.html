Manufacturing Location · Gujarat

Induction Base Plate Manufacturer
— Gujarat Unit, Sarigam

Fifty years of manufacturing discipline, now running across two states. Alongside our Mumbai base — founded in 1974 — Jagruti Steels manufactures SS 430 induction base plates and cookware components through our Gujarat unit — Mammal Industries, Sarigam — positioned close to India's largest aluminium cookware manufacturing cluster around Rajkot and Ahmedabad.

Why a Gujarat-Based Induction Plate Supplier Matters

The Rajkot-Ahmedabad belt is India's largest hub for aluminium non-stick and pressure cooker manufacturing — the majority of India's cookware brands source aluminium bodies from this cluster before bonding an induction base plate to complete the product. A Gujarat-based supply point cuts freight time and cost for these assemblers compared to sourcing the plate from Mumbai or further afield.

Our Gujarat unit, Mammal Industries in Sarigam, operates as a sister concern of Jagruti Steels Pvt. Ltd. — the same SS 430 material standard, the same in-house tooling discipline, and the same buyer drawing-first process used at our Mumbai facility, positioned where our cookware OEM customers need it most.

Gujarat Unit — Manufacturing Capacity

Full induction base plate and component manufacturing, supporting both standalone Gujarat orders and combined capacity with our Mumbai plant.
